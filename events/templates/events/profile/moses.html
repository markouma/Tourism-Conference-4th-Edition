MOSES MATSIKO BARYAMUJURA

FOUNDER AND CEO OF PINNACLE HOLDINGS LIMITED

He is an East African Business Entrepreneur, Founder and CEO of Pinnacle Holdings Limited since 2009, comprising of renowned and reputable security firms; Pinnacle Security Limited (16 years in existence) and Protectorate SPC Uganda Limited (26 years in existence) with subsidiaries in East Africa providing efficient and effective delivery of private security solutions and related services in the East African region as well the UAE, Iraq and other parts of the world.

Moses has also played a great role in Sports Tourism in Uganda and the continent. He is the immediate Past Uganda Golf Union President (2020-2024) with great achievements and milestones such as increased scholarship and enrollment opportunities for Ugandan young golfers to various colleges & universities in the USA among others. He is also the Director at Africa Golf Confederation in charge of Junior Golf Development, Uganda, Trustee Member of Uganda Taekwondo Federation and past Captain of Lake Victoria Kigo Golf Club.

He holds Master’s Degree in Security Crises and Risk Management from Kent University, United Kingdom and a Bachelor’s Degree in Mass Communication from Makerere University, Uganda.

In his career path, Moses consulted with Security Management Group LLC, based in Nevada State, USA for 4 years and later joined an American Leading Explosive Ordinance Detection Technologies (EODT) as the a Coordinator/Liaison Officer until 2009 where he was promoted as a Security Manager - Operational & Personnel Security Detail Center for Coalition Forces in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Additionally, he underwent specialized operational trainings in Nevada, USA, UAE and Iraq as the Critical Mission Support Services Expert and Country Security and Personnel Detail Manager in Iraq and was also a consultant for Joint Anti-Terrorism Task in Uganda from 2010 to 2012 and now a Council Member on an Inter-Forces Adhoc Committee responsible for evaluating preventive, reactive and post-reactive counter-terrorism measures in the region among others.

As a Security Consultant and Expert for many years due to great wealth of experience in security, Moses continues to be a Mentor & Influencer on security matters to the public & private sector and media in Uganda and world at large being was voted one of Uganda’s Top 60 Most Powerful & Influential Young Entrepreneurs and Corporate Executives in 2021.

Moses is known for his creative and proactiveness with utmost attention to detail & a hands-on approach coupled with pro-people skills, problem-solving and an innovative attitude to provide solutions to challenges related to security, stability and development thus able to wear many hats in one-go.

The many hats worn simultaneously include; Member of COUTA MICE Committee (A subsidiary of the Uganda Private Sector Foundation that creates awareness and promotes private sector investment in Uganda); Executive Member of The Uganda Private Security Association; Board Member of the PSL Holdings Limited; and Rotarian at Seven Lakes Rotary Club of Kigo to see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change across the globe.
