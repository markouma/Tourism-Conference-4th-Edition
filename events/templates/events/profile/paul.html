PAUL M. MUTUA

FOUNDER, STRATEGY AND MARKETING
DIRECTOR AT SPORTS TOURISM LIMITED

Founder, Strategy and Marketing Director at Sports Tourism Limited. A trained Digital Marketer and Certified Public Accountant.

More than 15 years of experience Managing Events, Sales & Marketing, Accounting, Operations and Merchandising at the Corporate Level. Experience acquired by working with leading organizations & hotels in Kenya including Sports Tourism Limited, Adujo Lodge & Campsite Limited, Mamba Village Centre, Wild-Waters Amusement Park, Neptune Hotels and Serena Group of Hotels.

Represented the private sector in several conferences and workshops including Sustainable Tourism Conference in Kenya, Unibran Sorghum Value Chain Agribusiness Incubation Network in Uganda, the 2022 Africities Convention in Kenya, the Inter Tourism Expo 2022 in Accra Ghana. Also organized the first ever Aviation and Tourism Sports Challenge in Mombasa bringing together aviation and Tourism stakeholders in a sporting event.

Through Sports Tourism Limited, offering Sports Logistics Services including accommodation, Transport, Venues, Match Ticketing and fan engagement for most of the Kenya Premier League Teams including AFC Leopards, Sofapaka FC as well as Nondescript RFC for rugby. Currently spearheading a #HonoringSportsLegends a program that recognizes, appreciates and brings together legendary sports figures. These are sports personalities who have made the country proud locally, regionally and on the international stage. Through his efforts in 2022 the Kenya Soccer Legends were able to play two friendly matches against their counterparts in Uganda. The initiative also helps in identifying, nurturing and promoting Youth Talents at an early stage and mentoring them to ensure they build great careers from their talents.

Actively spearheaded the Sports Tourism Limited’s #SportsForHealth campaign signing partnership MoUs with the County Government of Kisumu and County Government of Nakuru and other implementing partners. The campaign used Sports and Art to mobilize and sensitize Kenyans to take up the COVID-19 Vaccinations to help curb the Corona Virus pandemic.

Has received several Awards and Recognition both locally and internationally including the UN for spearheading the World Water Day celebrations. Selected in 2015 as a Youth Democracy Ambassador, a member of several business bodies including Tony Elumelu Entrepreneurship Alumni Network, the Google Hustle Academy Alumni Network and The Enablis Safaricom Foundation 2012 Business Plan Competition Top 100 in Kenya.
