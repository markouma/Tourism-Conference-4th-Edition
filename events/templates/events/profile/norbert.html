NORBERT TALAM, MBA, CPA-K, CPS-K

DIRECTOR GENERAL TOURISM REGULATORY
AUTHORITY

Mr. Norbert Talam is a visionary Finance Executive with over 25 years of experience in driving organizational growth, operational excellence, and institutional transformation. As Director General of the Tourism Regulatory Authority (TRA), he has played a pivotal role in reshaping Kenya’s tourism landscape through innovation, strong governance, and strategic partnerships.

With deep expertise in financial management, corporate governance, and public sector leadership, Mr. Talam is known for turning financial insights into strategy, strengthening systems, and fostering sustainable growth in the tourism sector.

Before joining TRA, he served as Chief Executive Officer, Company Secretary, and Head of Finance at First Community Bank, and held senior roles at K-Rep Bank (now Sidian Bank) and Kenya Airways (KQ).

A Certified Public Accountant (CPA–K) and Certified Public Secretary (CPS–K), Mr. Talam holds an MBA from the University of Nairobi and a Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting) from the Catholic University of Eastern Africa (CUEA). He is a member of ICPAK, ICPSK, and the Institute of Directors (IOD).

Under his leadership, TRA has achieved major milestones, including the National Tourism Accreditation Scheme, automation of tourism services, and review of the EAC classification criteria, positioning Kenya as a model in quality assurance and regional harmonization. His stewardship has also earned TRA continental recognition, with awards such as Africa Tourism Regulator of the Year (2023) and CEO of the Year (2024).

Mr. Talam’s leadership philosophy is anchored on sustainability, inclusivity, and collaboration, values that resonate strongly with the Uganda–Kenya Coast Tourism Conference’s vision of strengthening cross-border partnerships to unlock the full potential of East Africa’s tourism corridor.
