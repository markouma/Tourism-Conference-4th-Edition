OLIVIA NAKALEMBE

JOURNALIST

Olivia Nakalembe is a Ugandan journalist, moderator, and conservation advocate with a strong footprint in Tourism and Environmental communications. She holds a Bachelor's of Journalism and Communications from Makerere University with a major in Public Relations.

For over seven years, she worked with Next Media Services (NBS TV, Next Radio, Nile Post), where she was instrumental in elevating tourism and conservation from light features to serious editorial coverage. She has also contributed to Karibu Travel Magazine and other tourism platforms, telling stories that highlight Uganda’s culture, heritage, and natural beauty and tourism potential.

Areas of Expertise:
  • Conservation Journalism: Reporting and advocating for environmental protection and wildlife conservation.
  • Tourism Storytelling: Crafting narratives that showcase Uganda’s tourism and cultural heritage.
  • Travel Writing & Editorial Work: Producing and editing content on travel, culture, and community empowerment.
  • Moderation & Facilitation: Experienced moderator of panels in Uganda’s tourism private sector and under the Ministry of Tourism, Wildlife and Antiquities, with a focus on dialogue between stakeholders.
Achievements / Awards Won:
  • Wildlife Conservation Media Award 2024: Olivia Nakalembe won this for her media work, credited with repositioning tourism as a serious editorial subject in Uganda’s mainstream media by Prime Minister Hon. Robbinah Nabbanja.
  • Environment Journalist 2023 — National Environment Management Authority: Recognition for conservation coverage that protected and enriched the tourism value chain.
  • Uganda Biodiversity Fund Conservation Award: Overall journalist award for restoration of forest cover & improvement of livelihoods, handed over by Prime Minister Hon. Robinah Nabbanja.
  • Ekkula Sustainable Tourism Awards Nominee (2023): Recognized as Tourism Media Personality of the Year.
Professional and Personal Qualities:

Olivia is known for her strong communication skills, proficiency in moderating cross-sectoral discussions, and her commitment to using journalism not only to inform but also to inspire action. She effectively bridges advocacy and media, with a passion for sustainability, inclusivity, and youth engagement in tourism.

Currently, she serves as the communications officer at the Ministry of Tourism, Wildlife, and Antiquities in Uganda.

Role at the Uganda-Kenya Coastal Tourism Conference

As a moderator, Olivia will leverage her extensive experience in conservation storytelling, tourism media, and panel facilitation to guide discussions on sustainable tourism development, cross-border collaboration, cultural heritage preservation, and the promotion of coastal destinations.
