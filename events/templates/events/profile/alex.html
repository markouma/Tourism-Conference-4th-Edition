ALEX KAMUKAMA

CO-FOUNDER AKRIGHT CITY

Alex Kamukama a Co Founder of a Real Estate Development called Akright City is an accomplished Ugandan Investor and Business Executive.

While pursuing a Masters Degree in Business Administration from Humberside University in the UK, Alex was inspired to pursue a career in developing Orderly Human Settlements.

His masterly of Strategic Financial Planning and Management enabled him to support his Partner in mobilizing resources to set up the iconic Akright City worth Millions of Dollars.

Experience acquired from the many Boards he has served on makes Alex an accomplished corporate Governance practisioner.

Alex Kamukama as a Rotarian Leader has worked with Rotary Clubs to take humanitarian services to the less privileged.

He is Married to Rhona Kamukama, and between them, they have children and grandchildren.

Alex is a patriot and practising Christian who works hard to uphold Ugandas Motto; "For God and My Country".

He is a believer in the Statement by Napoleon Hill..."Action is the Real Measure of Intelligence"
