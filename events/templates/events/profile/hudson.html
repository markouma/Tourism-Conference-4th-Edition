HUDSON KARUME

EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND FOUNDER OF
VISION OF THE BLIND

Hudson Karume is a dedicated disability rights advocate and the Executive Director and Founder of Vision of the Blind, a national NGO he grew from a self-help group. Despite being born totally blind, he has forged a distinguished career championing the rights and socio-economic inclusion of Persons with Disabilities (PWDs).

Key Leadership & Achievements:

  • Political Representation: Served as the Nominated Member of the Mombasa County Assembly for PWDs (2013–2017), where he successfully drafted and passed motions on inclusive sports, accessible infrastructure, and the formation of the County Committee on Equal Opportunities.
  • Corporate Governance: Holds several board positions, including his current role as Board Chairperson of Chapakazi Transport Investors Mombasa, and previously served on the board of MOWASSCO (Mombasa Water Supply) among other several learning institutions boards.
  • Policy Influence: Instrumental in developing inclusive policies for both Mombasa County and the National Government and successfully influenced the Mombasa Water Board to integrate disability issues into its corporate policies. Through his strategic engagements with Mombasa County Government, Kenya Association of Hotel Keepers and Caterers (KAHC), and Organisations of Persons with Disabilities, he played a leading role in developing disability and inclusive employment policy, Mombasa County disability mainstreaming policy, and the County PWDs Empowerment Bill of 2017, among others.
  • Community Empowerment: Expanded Vision of the Blind's reach, securing employment and business opportunities for PWDs, promoting access to clean water, and assisting children with disabilities in accessing education and livelihoods through their caregivers.

Hudson is a proven leader, a successful entrepreneur with a disability, and a passionate champion for the rights of the girl-child and marginalized communities. He holds a Diploma in County Governance and Management from KCA University and is a Certified Director trained in corporate governance.

For more information: https://www.visionoftheblind.org
