Jacaranda Beach Resort

Jacaranda Beach Resort, Watamu stretches across nearly 32,000 square meters on the 1st row at The Jacaranda Bay. Its 149 rooms are spread out along more than 100 meters of beachfront and within a lush tropical garden. African-style buildings blend with Arab-inspired architecture, creating a striking contrast against the blue sky, while beachfront rooms are housed in blocks just steps from the ocean. Jacaranda is a place to be explored a unique combination of services and experiences unmatched along our coastline.

Ocean Beach Resort & SPA

Welcome to Ocean Beach Resort & SPA, celebrated as one the best hotels on Kenyan Coast and a standout among beach hotels in Malindi. Nestled along the serene sandy beach just 2 kilometers north of Malindi Town, our luxurious 5-star retreat offers elegantly designed accommodations surrounded by lush tropical gardens, creating a peaceful haven for our guests. Perfectly located near iconic attractions such as Hell’s Kitchen, Gede Ruins, and Watamu Marine Park, our resort is an ideal gateway for exploring Malindi’s cultural landmarks and Kenya’s captivating safaris.

The Semi-Olympic Pool invites guests to unwind with a leisurely swim or bask in the calming ambiance of Malindi’s radiant sun. Guests can also relax and rejuvenate at the Ocean Spa, renowned for its tranquil ambiance and revitalizing treatments. Delight your palate with exquisite dining options at Victoria Restaurant or enjoy a laid-back meal at the Dunes Beach Bar, each offering flavors that reflect the beauty.
